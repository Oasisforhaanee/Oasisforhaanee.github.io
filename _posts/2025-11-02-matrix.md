A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers, but it also carries deep meaning as a linear transformation.

## Matrix as Vectors

A vector `(1,1)` can be written as a column vector:

$$\mathbf{v}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\ 1\end{bmatrix}$$

## Basis Vectors

Standard basis vectors in `2D` are

$$\mathbf{i}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\ 0\end{bmatrix},\quad \mathbf{j}=\begin{bmatrix}0\\ 1\end{bmatrix}$$

## Column space and Rank

The column space is the set of all linear combinations of the columns.

The **rank** of a matrix is the dimension of its column space.

## Linear Transformation

Applying a matrix transforms space by rotation, shear, stretch, or projection.
